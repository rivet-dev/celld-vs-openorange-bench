Full run timeline

Measured Azure Blob requests priced at S3 rates (GET/HEAD $0.0004, PUT/LIST $0.005 per 1,000, deletes free), projected to 730 hours. Not an AWS or Azure bill. Compute, egress and storage are excluded; stored-byte snapshots price at $0.26/month or less.

Both platforms ran on the same Azure machines at each level (6 nodes at 8–10,000, 12 nodes at 15,000–20,000, 24 nodes at 25,000; 16 vCPU, 64 GB each). Rivet keeps 3 replicas of every write. Rivet Engine 3.0.0-preview.2, celld 0.6.3.

At 25,000 agents each Rivet Engine ran with TOKIO_WORKER_THREADS=1 to avoid a reply-admission race in the pinned release; earlier levels use the default.
